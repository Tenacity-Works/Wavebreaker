Core Combat Loop

Active Skill Meets Relentless Horde Survival

✦

Every champion brings bespoke weapon physics, active Ultimates, and build-defining runes—never mere reskins sharing the same projectile pool.

⚔

Precision or Auto-Aim

Aim twin-stick style with a high-contrast reticle, or press F at any moment to lock onto the nearest threat and focus purely on footwork and positioning.

✦

Rune Binding & Awakening

Bind two signature Weapon Runes at Levels 10 and 25, then Awaken their true forms at Levels 50 and 80—turning arrows into boomerangs or blizzards into projectile shields.

⚡

Illustrated Ultimates

Fill your Mana to unleash dragon-blade whirlwinds, soul rituals, and lightning storms. Every ultimate has a 3-second recast cooldown. Press R during gameplay to toggle splash animations on or off.

👑

Telegraphed Boss Sieges

Break 4-minute encirclement rings, outmaneuver elite champions, and survive 13-minute Final Bosses armed with telegraphed line charges, arcane circles, and ground slams.

Court of the Prince

Cross moonlit gothic ruins, dark ponds, and pine clearings while holding off the Undead Royal Legion—spear-throwing guards, frost knights, and armored Knight Commanders.

STAGE II Boss: The Warchief
Unlock: Survive 15m in Court of the Prince

Verdant Wilds

Navigate sunlit emerald clearings and ancient moss-draped trees swarming with goblin scouts, slingers, hex-casting shamans, and towering Brute Champions.

STAGE III Boss: The Frost King
Unlock: Survive 15m in Verdant Wilds • Unlocks Tier II Power-Ups

Frostpalm Reach

Follow snow-swept roads around eight frozen oasis lakes where ice golems, frost wolves, snow sorcerers, and yeti brutes guard the Frost King's domain.

STAGE IV Boss: The Empyrean Archon
Unlock: Survive 15m in Frostpalm Reach

Celestial Sanctum

Ascend into golden marble courtyards framed by winged angel statues and cascading waterfalls to face astral hippogriffs, oracle priests, pegasus lancers, and the Archon.

STAGE V Boss: The Sepulchral King
Unlock: Survive 15m in Celestial Sanctum

Mourning Hollow

Venture beyond the Sanctum into a haunted graveyard of tombstones, twisted dead trees, and drifting mist. Zombies, skeleton guards, ranged Gravecallers, floating skulls, and bone beasts hunt through the dark. Frequent minibosses make this the hardest realm yet.

Depth & Replayability

Tier II Power-Ups, Rerolls & Battlefield Drops

✦

Bank gold from every run—win or fall—to unlock new champions, expand permanent attributes up to Rank 10 after conquering Frostpalm Reach, toggle individual buffs freely, or refund your treasury anytime.

📈

Tier I & II Treasury

Invest gold across 12 core attributes, unlocking Ranks 6–10 upon clearing Frostpalm Reach, with full free Refund All and per-stat checkboxes.

🎲

Rarity Odds & Rerolls

Scale Luck to roll Rare, Epic, and 200%-potency Mythical level-up cards—and spend Rerolls (R) to hunt down your ideal build.

☀

Radiant Revival

Unlock Revival to choose a second wind upon falling, erupting in a golden light shockwave at 50% HP with 3 seconds of invulnerability.

🗺

Tactical Full Map

Pause at any time with M to chart Silver (+1 Level) and Bronze treasure caches across the arena, or toggle Tab to inspect stats and Ultimate details—even during level-up, rune, and awakening choices.

Ice Cream

Rare enemy drop that immediately restores 30% of Maximum Health in a burst of emerald light.

Arcane Magnet

Pulses across the battlefield, pulling every XP shard and gold coin on the entire map straight to you. XP is awarded as the shards arrive, so the XP bar fills along with the pull.

Siege Bomb

Unleashes a fiery shockwave that instantly obliterates all regular enemies on screen (sparing bosses & elites).

Development Update · 27 November 2026

Beyond the Sanctum

✦

Recent development changes for v0.1.1. Downloadable builds are published separately on GitHub Releases; check the release notes for what is included in each build.

☾

A Fifth Realm

Mourning Hollow brings undead hordes, necromancers, bone beasts, frequent minibosses, and the Sepulchral King. Smaller green projectiles leave more room to dodge. Court of the Prince also receives richer terrain and repaired map joins.

⚔

Sharper Combat

Caraxx’s continuous spin strikes 2.5 times per second at base Attack Speed. Danny’s ultimate ramp stops at +45%. All ultimates have a 3-second recast cooldown; R toggles their splash animations.

✿

Clearer Character Effects

Refreshed character poses, a seamless Caraxx spin, visible Windguard and Living Circuit shields, and stable Elys auras. Chiori’s body-centered 200-degree slash now follows her as she moves.

✦

More Readable Builds

Five blue Hypothermia orbs track Cyrene’s overflow Mana. Radiant Presence ticks four times per second. Common Damage upgrades grant +7.5 damage, Tier II prices grow more gently, and Tab remains available during upgrade choices.
